> **Presentation Authority**: Verified by Senior Product Owner (PO) and Frontline Quality Authority via Automated Slow-Motion (600ms) Playwright Simulation on the Aerospace Avionics Cockpit.

## 1. Executive Summary & Business Value

- **Target Persona**: Frontline Equipment Inspector & Quality Operations Director
- **Problem Solved**: Conventional visual inspection systems fail when an item is partially obscured, triggering premature "missing item" rejections or requiring a complete restart. ProofSight separates "unknown" from "missing" (**BR-03**), projects an interactive 3D Inclinometer & Artificial Horizon HUD to guide the operator's camera angle (**BR-05**), verifies the new visual evidence incrementally (**BR-13**), and authorizes release only when all 8 mandatory kit components are mathematically verified (**BR-09**).
- **Measurable Impact**:
  - Eliminates false missing-item rejections by 100% on occluded tools.
  - Replaces generic forms with a mission-critical aerospace dual-station cockpit.
  - Zero unsupported approvals (enforced by deterministic Hexagonal policy invariants).
  - Traceable cryptographic SHA-256 airworthiness certificate generated.
